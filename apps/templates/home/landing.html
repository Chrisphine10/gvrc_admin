What Hodi is

One trusted place to find help, wherever you are

Hodi is a directory and mobile app for people affected by gender-based violence in Kenya, and for the people supporting them. It lists services that have been checked by the Hodi team, shows how to reach them, and keeps emergency contacts one tap away.

Verified directory

Health, police, legal aid, counselling and shelters across Kenya, nearest first, with working contacts.

Maps and directions

Walking and driving routes with distance and time, and spoken guidance when looking at a screen is not safe.

Support, not judgement

A grounded chat assistant, guides on rights and safety planning, and calming audio for hard moments.

How it works

From "I need help" to the right door in four steps

Open Hodi

Install the app and start straight away. There is no sign-up and no account to create.

Say what you need

Choose a kind of help, such as medical care, police, legal aid, counselling or a safe place to stay.

See verified places near you

Hodi lists matching facilities nearest first, with opening status, contacts and services.

Call or go

Call with one tap or follow the route. The SOS button and Quick exit are always on screen.

Privacy & safety

Designed so that using Hodi does not put you at risk

No account needed

You do not give a name, phone number or email to use the app.

Quick exit

One button leaves Hodi instantly, in the app and on this page. Here you can also press Esc twice.

Reading stays private

Guides open inside the app, so they do not land in your browser history.

Shared phone?

Use a private browsing window for this site, and clear recent apps after using Hodi if someone checks your phone.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

I am in danger right now. What should I do?

Call 999 or 112 for the police, or 1195 for the national GBV hotline, which is free and open 24 hours. If you can, move to a place where other people are around.

Do I need to create an account?

No. The app works without an account, a name or a phone number.

Who decides which facilities are listed?

Hodi staff add each facility and check its details, services and contacts. When a facility closes or stops offering a service, its listing is updated.

Can someone see that I used Hodi?

Use Quick exit to leave immediately. On a shared phone, use a private browsing window for this site and remove Hodi from your recent apps. Guides open inside the app rather than in your browser.

Which languages does Hodi support?

English and Kiswahili.

How do I list or correct a facility?

Send the facility name, county and the change to the contact details below, and the Hodi team will check and update it.
